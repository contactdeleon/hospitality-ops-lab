Vacation Rental Turnover & Guest Readiness Checklist

A practical pre-arrival verification for short-term rental hosts and property managers. A property is not ready because cleaning is finished; it is ready when access, cleanliness, systems, inventory, amenities and known guest-impacting issues have been verified.

Reservation & access

Departure confirmed and turnover window assigned
Arrival time / early-arrival request reviewed
Door code, key, lockbox or access credentials tested
Parking and access instructions current
Guest-facing house information reviewed for accuracy

Turnover quality

Cleaning complete and independently inspected
Beds, linens and towels meet property standard
Kitchen, bathrooms and high-touch surfaces verified
Trash removed and exterior / entry presentation checked
Photo confirmation captured when required by policy

Systems & maintenance

HVAC operating at target setting
Wi-Fi tested
TVs, remotes and key guest-facing electronics checked
Plumbing, hot water and obvious leaks checked
Open maintenance items resolved or escalated before arrival

Amenities & inventory

Consumable pars restored
Kitchen, bath, coffee and laundry supplies checked as applicable
Pool, spa, grill and outdoor amenities inspected as applicable
Safety devices and required property notices visually checked
Missing or damaged items documented before guest arrival

Guest readiness

Welcome / arrival message sent on schedule
Known property limitation disclosed before it becomes a surprise
Emergency / support contact path is current
Property marked READY only after inspection is complete
