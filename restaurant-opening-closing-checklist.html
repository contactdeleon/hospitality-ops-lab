Restaurant Opening & Closing Checklist

Hospitality Ops Lab · FOH + BOH + manager verification

Restaurant: __________ Date: ______ Manager: __________

Opening — Manager

Review staffing, call-outs, reservations/events, large parties and expected volume.
Walk guest-facing areas for cleanliness, temperature, lighting, music, signage and safety issues.
Confirm POS, printers, handhelds, phones, Wi-Fi and payment systems are operational.
Review 86 list, specials, allergy notes, product shortages and priorities with FOH/BOH.

Opening — FOH

Dining room, host stand, bar, patio, restrooms and stations are cleaned and stocked.
Reservations, VIPs, celebrations, accessibility needs and large parties are reviewed.
Server sections and side-work assignments are posted and understood.

Opening — BOH

Required temperatures are checked and documented.
Prep list, pars, thawing, production priorities and expected covers are reviewed.
Stations are stocked, labeled, dated, rotated, sanitized and ready.

Shift Handoff

Open guest issues, comps/voids, equipment problems, staffing issues, 86 items and pending tasks are communicated.
Every unresolved item receives an owner and deadline.

Closing

Guest areas, stations, bar, host stand and restrooms are cleaned and reset.
All checks are closed; comps, voids, discounts and payment exceptions are reviewed.
Food is cooled, wrapped, labeled, dated, rotated and stored per the operation's food-safety procedures.
Tomorrow's prep needs, shortages, repairs and ordering concerns are communicated.

Final Manager Verification

Building, alarms, keys, exterior and restricted areas are checked.
Deposits/cash controls and labor/timekeeping exceptions are reviewed.
Manager completes a physical final walkthrough before sign-off.
