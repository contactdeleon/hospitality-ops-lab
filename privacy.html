Privacy

Hospitality Ops Lab is operated by Palm Springs Podcast Studio. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the website, contact us, purchase a product or use a service.

Information we may receive

We may receive your name, email address, company or property information, role, inquiry details, operating information you voluntarily provide, purchase status and service communications. Basic hosting logs may also record technical information such as IP address, browser type and access time.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Hospitality Ops Lab does not need or intend to receive your full payment-card number. Stripe may collect billing, payment and location information under its own privacy practices in order to process payments, prevent fraud and calculate tax where applicable.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, deliver purchased products, onboard and support customers, provide requested advisory work, maintain transaction and business records, improve our services, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support payment processing, email, hosting, file delivery and business operations, or when disclosure is required by law. We do not authorize service providers to use customer information for unrelated purposes on our behalf.

Operational and client data

Please limit business information to what is reasonably necessary for the requested work. Do not send unnecessary guest personally identifiable information, payment-card data, passwords, medical information or sensitive employee records. If sensitive information is required for a specific engagement, agree on an appropriate handling method first.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no email, website or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we control, where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for transaction, tax, fraud-prevention or legal-record purposes.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the business, website or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
