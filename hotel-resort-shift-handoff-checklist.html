Hotel & Resort Shift Handoff Checklist

Hospitality Ops Lab · Cross-department manager handoff

Property: __________ Date: ______ Shift: ______ Manager: __________

Occupancy & Arrivals

Review occupancy, arrivals, departures, stayovers, room moves and peak arrival windows.
Identify VIPs, owners, groups, celebrations, accessibility needs and special requests.
Review room-status exceptions, out-of-order rooms, early arrivals and late departures.

Guest Recovery

Review unresolved complaints, recovery commitments, compensation or amenity promises and deadlines.
Assign one accountable owner to every open guest issue.

Housekeeping & Rooms

Review clean/dirty/inspected room counts and rooms at risk of missing promised availability.
Confirm priority rooms, rush rooms, linen/supply constraints and maintenance findings.

Engineering & Facilities

Review open work orders affecting rooms, public areas, elevators, HVAC, water, lighting or safety.
Identify outages or limitations requiring guest communication or workarounds.

Food & Beverage

Review outlet hours, reservations, events, staffing and high-volume periods.
Communicate shortages, 86 items, special menus, allergies and service limitations.
Confirm room service, pool service, banquet, amenity or VIP delivery priorities.

Pool, Recreation & Amenities

Confirm opening status, staffing, towels, seating, cleanliness, equipment and safety readiness.
Review scheduled activities, owner events, family programming and weather impacts.

Final Handoff

Outgoing manager identifies the top three operational risks.
Every unresolved item has an owner, next action and deadline.
Incoming manager confirms understanding of guest-impacting priorities.
