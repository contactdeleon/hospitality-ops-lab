Illustrative opportunity

This number is simply the selected percentage of current labor spend. It does not assume layoffs or lower service standards. In practice, opportunities may come from better deployment, scheduling, overtime control, opening/closing coverage, cross-training, timekeeping discipline and reduced rework.

Use this number correctly

A 1%–5% improvement is not automatically achievable. The value of the calculation is to show whether recurring operational friction is economically large enough to justify management attention. Validate assumptions against your own labor model, service standards and applicable employment requirements.
